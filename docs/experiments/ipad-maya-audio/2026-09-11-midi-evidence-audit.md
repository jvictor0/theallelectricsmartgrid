The broad statement that MIDI/controller activity was removed and failures persisted needs qualification. The experiments do not jointly exclude all MIDI-related work.

1. September9 WB physically absent: six matched MAYA0x82 transaction errors, callback gaps, xr increments and analog interruptions in244.629seconds before USB-enumeration-confirmed WB attachment22:25:22.419. One more in131.260seconds after. Nominal throughout; directly rechecked app log thermal0 at all seven callback gaps and all35,043records in the rounded22:21:18–22:27:33 bounds. Full precise report has35,061captured callbacks. The MIDI sender remained running; absent WB does not remove worker wakeups, message generation/queues or prove all other routes silent. This is strong evidence WB physical USB traffic is not necessary for these long failures, not an exclusion of MIDI software load or contribution to incidence. Raw report copied to2026-09-09-wb-disconnect-result.md; its figure links remain relative to original raw directory /private/tmp/smartgrid-ipad-no-controller-analysis.

It excludes the dedicated worker as necessary in that condition, not MIDI output generally. It removes both polling and basic-message output while preserving direct SysEx.

Heavy MIDI activity is compatible with clean measured playback, but cannot be excluded as a probabilistic contributor. Before restart the user reported absent LEDs despite submitted counters; do not equate API submission with physical device acknowledgement.

Current code: regular audio-frame WB/Launchpad SysEx and short-message bypasses use MidiSender queues. SendMessage and SendSysex discard before queueing when SMARTGRID_MIDI_SEND_THREAD=0. Direct lower-level MidiOutputHandler::SendMessage still calls JUCE sendMessageNow; normal frame traffic reaches it from worker. Control-thread handshake and final LED clearing remain direct. No completed post-migration measured worker-off trial was found in the journal. Therefore earlier worker-off results must not be attributed to the stronger present-day flag. No global final-submission counter proves all-route zero MIDI traffic in those historical trials.

Logical gap: WB absent test retained worker; worker-off test retained direct WB traffic. Those two separate observations do not exclude a MIDI subsystem contribution through either path. Other-app controls do not match SmartGrid MIDI workload. Proven narrower statements: physical WB not necessary for tested long failures; original worker not necessary for either symptom with direct SysEx still enabled; audio-thread SysEx submission not necessary for long failures after migration. All-MIDI-off remains untested as a verified combined condition.
