# September 13: post-merge MAYA run with failures, then a long clean run

All times are September 13, 2026, Pacific Daylight Time. These are two observational SmartGridOne runs on the iPad through the USB hub with MAYA44 USB+ and WRLD.BLDR. They were not controlled A/B trials, and the retained evidence does not by itself establish why the failure rate changed.

## 3:54–4:39 PM app session; 4:19–4:34 PM failure cluster

- The app launched at 15:54:05, was quit from the app switcher at 15:54:18, and relaunched at 15:54:38. The analyzed process log begins at 15:54:39. Its first MAYA transaction failure was at 16:19:14, approximately 24 minutes 36 seconds after relaunch.
- The app requested 48,000 Hz and 512 frames. At 15:54:49 it briefly rejected a 44,100 Hz / 512-frame format during configuration. Subsequent minute snapshots on MAYA reported an actual 48,000 Hz / 512-frame route with four inputs and four outputs. The MIDI sender logged `real_time=0` at process start; this log alone does not establish the I/O thread's sleep interval or exact installed binary hash.
- From 16:19:14.024 through 16:33:51.729, the kernel recorded **13 MAYA input endpoint 0x82 transaction errors**, each status `0xe00002ed`. Each matched an `AUAInputTransferManager` status error and a CoreAudio I/O restart, followed by a 250 ms USB-audio lock delay. No matching MAYA output-endpoint transaction error was found in this cluster.
- App `long_gaps` rose from 0 to 13, and `xruns` from 2 to 15, during the same cluster. Logged last-gap durations were about 317–327 ms. The 2 initial xruns and 2 format mutes preceded the failure cluster; do not count them as cluster failures. Instrumented processing overruns remained at 1 after startup.
- The app logged nominal thermal state (0) through 16:25:53, then serious (2) beginning 16:26:09. Seven transaction errors preceded that transition and six followed it. Thermal state returned to nominal by 16:38:24. This run does not show serious state preventing failures.
- iPad `symptomsd` power snapshots reported external power connected throughout the failure cluster. At 16:19:55 the battery was shown as 100% and `battery-charging=1`; by the next 20-second snapshot at 16:20:15, `battery-charging=0`, and it remained 0 through at least 16:39. The first three MAYA errors occurred while the charging flag was 1; the remaining ten occurred after it switched off. These flags establish reported battery charging state, not charging current, PD watts, or MAYA supply voltage. [Power snapshots](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1515-1645/power-1615-1640.log).
- SmartGridOne's internal recording began at 16:17:55.270 and ended at approximately 16:34:51, spanning all 13 errors. The iPad file was named `recording-2026-09-13T161755.270.wav` (about 12.53 GB). Its 4 KiB header was saved locally; the full recording was not copied or waveform-analyzed for this note. The earlier user listening observation that internal recordings lack the audible glitch remains separate evidence.

Evidence: [app timing extract](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1515-1645/app-timing-episode.log), [MAYA system episode](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1515-1645/system-maya-episode.log), [full app log](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1515-1645/2026-09-13T15-54-39-079.log), and [system archive](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1515-1645/system.logarchive). ## 7:30–9:14 PM app session; no settled-run USB failure

- The collected system archive covers the requested period starting at 19:15. App logging first appears in a short process at 19:29:58, followed by the analyzed process beginning 19:30:29. The iPad enumerated the hub and peripherals at about 19:30:24–29. The app was user-quit at 21:14:36, for an analyzed process lifetime of about 1 hour 44 minutes.
- On WRLD.BLDR enumeration, the kernel reported **3 endpoint 0x00 transaction errors** at 19:30:24.927, 19:30:25.031, and 19:30:25.133, plus a remote-wake compliance warning. These were connection-time control errors, not MAYA audio-transfer errors; no further WRLD transaction errors were found in the run.
- MAYA had several configuration-time audio restarts between 19:30:30 and 19:30:34, including zero-length-packet/lock-delay messages and aborted outstanding requests. These were startup events, not `0xe00002ed` transaction-status errors. The app had one 45.674 ms `long_gap` and 2 xruns by 19:31:30; neither counter increased again through its final diagnostic snapshot at 21:14:21. Processing overruns stayed at 1 and format mutes at 0.
- **No MAYA input or output transaction errors, CoreAudio I/O restarts, USB-audio lock-delay events, or logged driver timestamp-drift events occurred during the settled 19:31–21:14 run.** The iPad USB-subsystem scan also found no unlabelled mid-run hub/port errors. The app reported actual 48,000 Hz / 512 frames, four inputs/four outputs on MAYA, nominal thermal state (0), and zero native MIDI errors throughout. It reported `preferred_rate=45100` in minute diagnostics while the actual session/device rate remained 48,000; that preferred-value discrepancy is an observation, not evidence of an active 45.1 kHz route.
- At 21:14:35, the system aborted outstanding MAYA requests and restarted I/O as the user quit SmartGridOne. The audio session then deactivated and the system route reverted to 44,100 Hz. This is teardown activity, not a spontaneous failure during playback.

Evidence: [app log](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1915-2115/2026-09-13T19-30-28-853.log), [USB-subsystem extract](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1915-2115/usb-events.log), [end-transition extract](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1915-2115/end-transition.log), and [system archive](/private/tmp/sgo-20260913-1915-2115/system.logarchive). The archive viewer warned of a wall-clock adjustment when queried with `--end`; timestamps above were cross-checked against app logs and the separate end-transition query. This was a log-based check, not an analog-output recording, so it cannot exclude audible artifacts without USB errors or app counter changes.

## Comparison

The afternoon episode reproduces the established failure chain: MAYA input transfer error → driver I/O restart/lock delay → roughly 320 ms app callback gap. The evening run shows that the same iPad/MAYA topology can remain free of that logged chain for about 1 hour 43 minutes after startup, even though initial enumeration was noisy. The difference is real within the captured logs, but neither run isolates its cause. In particular, startup aborts and the user-quit restart in the evening should not be grouped with spontaneous mid-session `0xe00002ed` failures.
